# Traceability: repairs/1

One row per requirement ID `contracts/repairs-1.md` defines. Format:
`conformance/traceability/README.md`.

**Every row is `TBD-wave1`, and the reason is sequencing rather than oversight.**
This contract is published as TEXT ahead of its implementation — its four shape
questions (where an issue lives, what a detector is, what a remediation is, and
whether the pack-facing verb is in scope) were decided, and the section is up for
review before anything is built against it. There is no case to list yet, and
naming one would be the overclaim this column exists to prevent.

Two rows will resist a corpus case even after the surface exists, and it is
worth saying which before a later reader takes a `covered` for more than it
means. **REP-003** forbids secret material in an issue's prose; no case can
enumerate what a future detector might place in a string, so it is enforceable by
review of what the platform's own detectors emit rather than by a case. **REP-032**
requires a detector's own failure to raise an issue rather than return zero — a
case can present one broken detector, which proves that detector reports its
failure, not that every future one will.
